About this game

Escape the Carceri is a free, single-page educational game built around Giovanni Battista Piranesi's Le Carceri d'Invenzione (Imaginary Prisons), a series of etchings begun in 1745 and reworked in 1761. The puzzles are grounded in plate titles and standard museum descriptions — stairs, towers, chains, machinery, scale, and confinement.

The game is provided by the CrimRxiv Consortium. Everything runs in your browser. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no data leaves your device.

What you'll do

  1. Read the inscription on Piranesi's title plate.
  2. Identify visible architectural features.
  3. Match clues to the right plate.
  4. Order means of restraint by severity.
  5. Find a detail with a region hotspot.
  6. Fill in plate titles.
  7. Compare two plates side by side.
  8. Spot the odd plate out.
  9. Set a final lock to escape.

How to play

  • You'll move through sixteen plates from Piranesi's Carceri.
  • Every puzzle is click-only. There's nothing to type until the end.
  • Stuck? Hit Hint. It won't penalize you.
  • Wrong answers don't end the game — you can keep trying.
  • At the end you'll generate a printable certificate. Your name stays in your browser.
  • Want to skip the puzzles? Open the Gallery to browse all 16 plates.
